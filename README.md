# My Favorite K-Drama: *Goblin* 

## 📺 Overview
**Goblin** (also known as *Guardian: The Lonely and Great God*) is a fantasy-romance K-drama that aired on **tvN** from *December 2016 to January 2017*. It has **16 episodes** and was written by Kim Eun-sook.

It tells the story of ~~an ordinary man~~ **Kim Shin**, a goryeo-era general cursed to live forever as an immortal goblin. Only his destined bride can end his curse.

---

## 👥 Main Characters
| Character | Actor | Role |

| Grim Reaper | Lee Dong-wook | A reaper who lives with Kim Shin |
| Sunny | Yoo In-na | Chicken restaurant owner |
| Yoo Deok-hwa | Yook Sung-jae | Heir of the family serving Kim Shin |
